## Промпты

Каждый запрос состоял из общего промпта и соответствующего описания предмета ниже. Параметр генерации: `transparent_background: true`.

Общий промпт:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: single small UI illustration for Russian civic map app Gorod 21, belongs to a consistent set of six. Create a minimal chunky 3D rubber/plastic icon in the style of the user's references: vivid azure and royal blue object, smooth rounded inflated forms, very few details, soft highlights and simple studio shading. Center it on a pale icy blue rounded square tile with softly bevelled edges. Square composition, tile occupies 90% of canvas, entire tile visible with comfortable transparent margins. Object fills 65% of tile. Nearly front-on view with subtle three-quarter volume. Palette blue #2376eb #1688ef #70c8ff and pale #dcefff only, no red orange or metallic colors. TRUE transparent background outside the tile. No words, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks, no symbols added around it, no visual clutter, no strong glow, no harsh light.

Отключение воды:

> Subject: a simple blue water faucet with a T-handle and one single water droplet below its spout. No extra ring hanging from the faucet. Water outage scenario.

Ремонт освещения:

> Subject: one simple rounded blue street lamp with a short curved neck, domed shade and pale blue bulb, with three tiny chunky blue light rays below. Lighting repair scenario.

Ремонт дороги:

> Subject: a short blue road slab seen in gentle perspective, two pale blue lane dashes, with a chunky blue masonry trowel resting on a simple smooth blue patch. Road repair scenario. Minimal ground detail.

Граффити:

> Subject: one chunky blue spray paint can tilted slightly to the right, round nozzle, and three small rounded blue spray drops. Graffiti on a building facade scenario. No actual text graffiti.

Оледенение:

> Subject: one thick chunky blue six-armed snowflake with short rounded branches. A simple perfectly recognizable symmetrical snowflake. Road icing scenario.

Ремонт теплосети:

> Subject: a compact blue heating radiator of four rounded vertical tubes with short connecting pipes and one chunky blue wrench diagonally in front. Heating network repair scenario. No extra objects.
